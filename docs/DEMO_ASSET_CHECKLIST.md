Also record a 60–90 second screen capture of the recommended story in [DEMO_RUNBOOK.md](DEMO_RUNBOOK.md). Start it on the dashboard, use the prepared notice, apply it, show Updates and Plan, and finish on the AI answer. Avoid recording secrets, OAuth pages, terminal history, or Gmail inbox content.

If an online dependency fails during judging, say: “The local core is working; I’ll use the recorded flow for the optional AI/extraction step and continue with the live deterministic dashboard.”
